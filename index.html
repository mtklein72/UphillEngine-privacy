UphillEngine Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

UphillEngine helps you record and compare uphill activities. This policy describes the app's use of information on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Information used by the app

With your permission, UphillEngine uses location and motion measurements to record routes, elevation and activity. It can read Apple Health workouts, routes and heart-rate information you choose to import, and write completed workouts and associated measurements to Apple Health when permitted. It can also receive heart rate from a Bluetooth sensor and workout measurements from your Apple Watch.

Your routes, workout history, settings and local diagnostic information are stored on your devices. The app does not require an UphillEngine account or operate an UphillEngine server for this information. Device backups and Apple Health synchronization are controlled by your Apple settings.

Maps and other services

The app uses Apple Maps and location services to display maps, search for places and suggest locations. Apple may process related requests under its own privacy policies. If you choose to check an official uphill route, the app requests the relevant resort's public website; that website may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. If you choose to export or share a recovery copy, information is sent to the destination you select.

Your choices

You can control location, motion, Bluetooth and Health permissions in iPhone settings and Health. You can remove locally saved sessions and routes in the app. Imported Apple Health workouts are preserved when removed from app history; deleting a workout recorded by UphillEngine may also remove its linked Health workout. Independent Health measurements can remain in Apple Health. Apple and your selected sharing destinations have their own controls and retention practices.
